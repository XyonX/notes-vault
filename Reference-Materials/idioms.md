# ✅ **Top 40 Most Commonly Asked Idioms (Placement & Job Tests)**

### ⭐ _Must-Know Idioms (Very Frequent)_

1. **Back to square one**  
    → Start again from the beginning.
    
2. **Break the ice**  
    → Start a conversation or make people comfortable.
    
3. **Hit the nail on the head**  
    → Say exactly the right thing.
    
4. **A piece of cake**  
    → Something very easy.
    
5. **Once in a blue moon**  
    → Happens rarely.
    
6. **Beat around the bush**  
    → Avoid talking directly; not coming to the point.
    
7. **Cut corners**  
    → Do something cheaply or carelessly.
    
8. **Burn the midnight oil**  
    → Work late into the night.
    
9. **Under the weather**  
    → Feeling slightly sick.
    
10. **Let the cat out of the bag**  
    → Reveal a secret by mistake.
    

---

### ⭐ _Medium Difficulty (Common in English MCQs)_

11. **Cost an arm and a leg**  
    → Very expensive.
    
12. **Hit the sack**  
    → Go to sleep.
    
13. **By the book**  
    → Follow rules exactly.
    
14. **Call it a day**  
    → Stop working for now.
    
15. **To be in hot water**  
    → To be in trouble.
    
16. **The ball is in your court**  
    → It’s your decision or responsibility now.
    
17. **Bite off more than you can chew**  
    → Take on too much work.
    
18. **On cloud nine**  
    → Extremely happy.
    
19. **Break a leg**  
    → Good luck (informal, often used in performance contexts).
    
20. **Cry over spilled milk**  
    → Worry about something that cannot be changed.
    

---

### ⭐ _Slightly Advanced (Good for SDE interviews)_

21. **Throw in the towel**  
    → Give up; admit defeat.
    
22. **On the same page**  
    → Agree or understand the same thing.
    
23. **Go the extra mile**  
    → Do more than expected.
    
24. **Think outside the box**  
    → Think creatively.
    
25. **Under the table**  
    → Done secretly, usually illegal.
    
26. **A blessing in disguise**  
    → Something that seems bad but turns out good.
    
27. **Hit the road**  
    → Leave or start a journey.
    
28. **Pull someone’s leg**  
    → Joke or tease someone.
    
29. **Burn bridges**  
    → Damage relationships permanently.
    
30. **In the long run**  
    → Over a long period of time.
    

---

### ⭐ _Business / Corporate Idioms (Useful for HR Round)_

31. **Touch base**  
    → Talk briefly or check in with someone.
    
32. **In the loop**  
    → Keep updated.
    
33. **Get the ball rolling**  
    → Start a project or discussion.
    
34. **Up to speed**  
    → Having the latest information.
    
35. **Silver lining**  
    → A good thing in a bad situation.
    
36. **On the back burner**  
    → Delayed or postponed.
    
37. **Game changer**  
    → Something that changes the situation completely.
    
38. **At the end of the day**  
    → Ultimately / in conclusion.
    
39. **Raise the bar**  
    → Increase the standards or expectations.
    
40. **In a nutshell**  
    → In short / summary.
