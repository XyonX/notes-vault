# ✅ Subject–Verb Agreement with _Do_ / _Does_

## 📌 When to Use

- **Do** → with **I, You, We, They**
    
- **Does** → with **He, She, It**
    

Used for **negatives** and **questions** in the present simple tense.

---

## 🛑 Negatives

**Pattern:**  
`Subject + do/does + not + base verb`

Examples:

- I **don’t** play football.
    
- They **don’t** eat meat.
    
- He **doesn’t** like apples.
    
- She **doesn’t** study here.
    

👉 Always use the **base form** of the verb after _do/does not_.

---

## ❓ Questions

**Pattern:**  
`Do/Does + subject + base verb + ... ?`

Examples:

- Do you play football?
    
- Do they eat meat?
    
- Does he like apples?
    
- Does it work properly?
    

---

## ✅ Affirmatives (No Do/Does)

- I play football.
    
- They eat meat.
    
- He **likes** apples.
    
- She **studies** here.
    

👉 With **he/she/it**, verb takes **-s/-es** in affirmative.

---

## ❌ Common Mistakes

- He **don’t like** → ❌ should be **He doesn’t like**
    
- He **doesn’t likes** → ❌ should be **He doesn’t like**
    
- **Does he likes?** → ❌ should be **Does he like?**
    

---

## 🔑 Quick Reference Table

|Subject|Affirmative|Negative|Question|
|---|---|---|---|
|I / You / We / They|I like apples.|I don’t like apples.|Do you like apples?|
|He / She / It|He likes apples.|He doesn’t like apples.|Does he like apples?|

---

## 🧭 Quick Flow (Cheat Rule)

- **If subject = He/She/It** → use **Does/Doesn’t**
    
- **If subject = I/You/We/They** → use **Do/Don’t**
    
- After **do/does** → verb always stays **base form**
