### 🔤 The Rule:

Use **“a”** before words that begin with a **consonant sound**, Use **“an”** before words that begin with a **vowel sound**.

### 🧠 Now the twist:

# It’s **not** about the **letter** the word starts with—it's about the **sound**.


### Articles

1. **Indefinite Articles (a, an)**
    
    - Used with singular countable nouns when the listener doesn’t know which specific thing is meant.
        
    - _a_ → before consonant sounds (a book, a university)
        
    - _an_ → before vowel sounds (an apple, an hour)
        
2. **Definite Article (the)**
    
    - Refers to something specific or already known.
        
    - _the moon, the sun, the Taj Mahal_
        
    - With superlatives (_the best, the tallest_)
        
    - With unique things (_the internet, the Prime Minister_)
        
3. **Omission of Articles**
    
    - No article before plural or uncountable nouns used in a general sense (_Books are useful. Water is essential._)
        
    - No article before proper nouns (_India, Mount Everest_) unless emphasizing uniqueness (_the Himalayas, the Ganga_).
