## ✅ Final List with Correct Articles + Explanations

- **a university**  
    ✔ _“university”_ starts with **/juː/** (a consonant “yu” sound), so it takes **a**, not _an_.
    
- **an hour**  
    ✔ “h” is silent → sounds like _our_, so **an**.
    
- **a European country**  
    ✔ _European_ = /juː/ sound → consonant → **a**, not _an_. But when we mean _a specific one_, we’d say _the European country you mentioned_.
    
- **an honest man**  
    ✔ “h” is silent → sounds like _onest_, so **an**.
    
- **a one-eyed man**  
    ❌ Your guess was _an_, but it’s actually **a one-eyed man**.  
    ✔ Reason: _one_ starts with the consonant sound /w/, like “won.”
    
- **an umbrella**  
    ✔ Classic vowel sound → **an**.
    
- **a hotel**  
    ✔ “hotel” has /h/ pronounced → **a hotel**.  
    (But in older British English, sometimes _an hotel_ was used — rare today.)
    
- **an MLA**  
    ✔ Pronounced _em-el-ay_ → starts with vowel sound /e/ → **an**.
    
- **an heir**  
    ✔ “h” silent → sounds like _air_ → **an**.
    
- **the USA**  
    ✔ Countries with abbreviations (USA, UAE, UK) use **the** because they’re like proper titles.
    
- **the UK**  
    ✔ Same reason → **the UK**.
    
- **the Ganga**  
    ✔ Rivers always take **the** (the Nile, the Amazon, the Ganga).
    
- **Mount Everest** (❌ no article!)  
    ✔ Mountains usually don’t take _the_ (Mount Everest, Mount Fuji).  
    ❗ But ranges do: _the Himalayas_.
    
- **the sun / the moon**  
    ✔ Unique heavenly bodies → **the**.
    
- **the earth**  
    ✔ When referring to our planet, **the earth**.  
    (But in scientific context, sometimes just “Earth” without _the_.)
    
- **the sky / the sea**  
    ✔ Natural unique phenomena → **the**.
    
- **(no article) gold / milk / information**  
    ✔ Uncountable nouns in general sense → no article.  
    (_some milk, a piece of information_ if you want quantity).
    
- **the best player in the team**  
    ✔ Superlative adjective → always **the**.
    
- **the most beautiful girl in the class**  
    ✔ Superlative/most-phrase → **the**.
    
- **Man is mortal** (❌ no article)  
    ✔ When speaking of mankind in general → no article.  
    (If we said _The man you met is mortal_, then article is needed.)
    
- **Books are useful** (❌ no article)  
    ✔ Plural noun in general sense → no article.
    
- **Water is essential** (❌ no article)  
    ✔ Uncountable in general sense → no article.

## 🌟 Quick Key Patterns

- **a/an** → depends on **sound**, not spelling.
    
- **the** → unique things, superlatives, rivers, seas, ranges, countries with abbreviations.
    
- **no article** → plural/uncountable nouns in general sense, mankind in general.
