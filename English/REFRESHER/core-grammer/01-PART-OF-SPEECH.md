Noun- Names of people, places, things, ideas (teacher, city, book, freedom).

Adjective - describes noun (beautifull , tall , red)

pronoun- Replaces noun ( he , she, it , they)

Verb - Actions being words

Adverb- describes verb ()

- **Quickly** – _She ran quickly to catch the bus._
    
- **Carefully** – _He painted the wall carefully._
    
- **Loudly** – _The baby cried loudly._

Preposition - Shows relation (in, on, at, by, with, to).

conjunction : Joins words/clauses (and, but, because).

**Interjection** → Sudden expression (oh!, wow!, alas!).
